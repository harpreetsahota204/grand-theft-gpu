## Rules of the run

- You are working alone in this directory. Everything you build lives here.
- This is a Vite project. After every one of your turns, the harness runs `npm install` and `npm run build`, then loads `dist/index.html` in a browser. **If the build fails, your game counts as broken for that turn.** Keep the build green.
- **You can see your game.** `tools/screenshot` builds it the same way, plays it in a headless browser and saves a PNG you can open with the Read tool. `tools/screenshot --hold w:2000` holds a key first; `tools/screenshot --help` lists the options.
- **The run continues until the budget runs out.** After each of your turns, a critic (a fresh session of you, with no memory of building the game) plays it, reviews it against this brief and writes your next instructions. Each critic sees the last review's notes and checks whether you fixed them, so a note you skip comes back. Reaching Tier 4 doesn't end the run: past the ladder, the critic asks for ambition, and there is always more to make better.
- **Reviews are how the game gets better.** End your turn whenever you want a review. Each one tells you what's broken, and each one is paid from your budget: the spend you're shown includes the critics. Smaller turns get more reviews, and a game that breaks after a long, expensive turn may leave no money to fix it. Amazing comes from many reviewed turns, not one big one. Keep a short `DONE.md` that says what the game is, how to play it, what you changed this turn and, for each tier you've reached, the keys and route that get there from a fresh load. The critic follows it, so keep it current as the game grows.
- **Don't break what works.** Before you end a turn, replay the routes in `DONE.md` (yourself or with the playtester). A critic puts anything that worked before and now doesn't at the top of its list, and a tier only counts while nothing below it has regressed.
